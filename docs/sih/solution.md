# Solution

FarmFlow AI creates a direct agri marketplace where farmers and FPOs can list produce, AI can forecast local demand, and buyers can source high-quality produce at transparent prices. The platform minimizes intermediaries while preserving a data-driven decision layer for ordering, route planning, and trust.

## Direct chain model

- listing and discoverability
- AI-based demand scoring and price guidance
- buyer-farmer matching engine
- order aggregation across multiple farmers
- route optimization and tracking
- transparent cost and margin visibility

## Value

- better farmer earnings
- lower consumer prices
- shorter logistics distances
- lower waste and stronger trust
