# Problem Statement

Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices. FarmFlow AI addresses this gap with a direct agricultural supply-chain platform focused on trust, price transparency, and farmer empowerment.

## Key challenges

- fragmented supply and weak buyer discovery
- limited forecasting for crop demand
- opaque pricing across multiple channels
- high transportation costs from unoptimized routes
- food wastage due to poor timing and matching

## SIH context

This solution is aligned with the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution theme for Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development.
