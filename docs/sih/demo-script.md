# Demo Script

1. Open the landing page and click Start SIH Demo.
2. Farmer logs in as Raj Kumar and lists 1,200 kg tomatoes.
3. AI predicts demand of 16,100 kg and recommended price ₹22–24/kg.
4. Buyer logs in and searches the marketplace.
5. Place an order for 1,000 kg tomatoes.
6. Matching engine identifies three nearby farmers.
7. Aggregation creates one consolidated shipment.
8. Logistics engine shows optimized route and savings.
9. Price transparency screen highlights farmer gain and consumer saving.
10. Traceability timeline confirms the route from farm to buyer.
